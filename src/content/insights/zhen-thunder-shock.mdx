# Zhen: Thunder, Shock, Awakening

Zhen, the 51st hexagram, is thunder above thunder. It speaks to sudden movement, shocking revelation, and the awakening that comes from disruption.

## The Shock That Reveals

Thunder doesn't ask permission. It arrives suddenly, breaking patterns, and awakens. In that moment of disruption, something new becomes visible.

## Opportunity often reveals itself in moments of shock.

## From Disruption to Creation

What feels like a crisis can be the moment that clears space for something new. The old structures fall away, and we see clearly what was hidden.
