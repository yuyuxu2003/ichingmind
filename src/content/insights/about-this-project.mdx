# About This Project

The I Ching can be read not merely as an oracle, but as a language of situations — a way of observing what is emerging, what is unstable, what calls for restraint, and what invites movement.

This project is an exploration of that perspective.

## Why This Exists

Too often, the I Ching is dismissed as superstition or reduced to a simple fortune-telling device. But beneath that surface lies a sophisticated system of thought about change, timing, and human affairs.

I created IChingMind to share a different way of reading this ancient text — one that is practical, thoughtful, and useful for modern decision-making.

## A Framework, Not a Fortune-Teller

The hexagrams of the I Ching describe patterns of change. They don't predict the future; they describe the present with remarkable clarity. When you understand the pattern you're in, you can make better decisions about how to move forward.

This is the core insight that animates everything you'll find here.
