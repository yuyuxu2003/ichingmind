# Xun: Wind, Penetration, Circulation

Xun, the 57th hexagram, is composed of wind above wind. It speaks to penetration, subtle influence, and the way information and value move through systems.

## The Logic of Wind

Wind doesn't force its way; it finds the cracks. It penetrates where doors are open, carrying pollen, scent, and information across distances.

In modern terms, this describes how ideas spread, how markets form, and how influence works in networked systems.

## Information as a Force

When we read Xun in a business context, we see the logic of marketing, communication, and distribution. It's about getting the right message to the right place at the right time.
